Junho Oh (Undergraduate student) won the Encouragement Award (학부생부문 장려상) at the Annual Domestic Korea Computer Congress Conference (KCC 2026) for his paper _"Performance Analysis of Least Request across One-to-Many and Many-to-Many Communication Patterns in CPU Heterogeneous Environments"_.
